# UB-HMS defect log

Defects found by the automated unit tests, end-to-end tests and manual checks during development.
Each entry records how it was found and how it was fixed and retested. This log is the source for
Chapter 7 (System Test Plan), section 7.4.

Severity: Critical (security or data loss), High (feature unusable), Medium (feature wrong in some cases), Low (cosmetic).

| ID | Sprint | Found by | Severity | Description | Steps to reproduce | Root cause | Resolution | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| DEF-001 | 1 | UT-AUT-004 | Medium | Account locked after 4 wrong passwords instead of 5. | Enter a wrong password four times, then check `locked_until`. | In the SQL `UPDATE`, `failed_attempts` was incremented before `locked_until` was calculated, and MySQL/MariaDB evaluate `SET` clauses left to right, so the lock test saw the new count. | `locked_until` is now assigned before `failed_attempts` in `includes/auth.php`. | Fixed, UT-AUT-004 passes |
| DEF-002 | 1 | UT-AUT-007 | High | The per-computer sign-in throttle never blocked anyone. | Make 20 failed sign-ins from one address, then sign in correctly from it. | `audit()` recorded the web server's own view of the address instead of the address given to `attempt_login()`, so the throttle query found no matching rows. | `audit()` now accepts the address explicitly and `attempt_login()` passes it. | Fixed, UT-AUT-007 passes |
| DEF-003 | 1 | E2E-025 to E2E-027 | Low | A patient with no middle name was displayed as "Amani  Kiplagat" (two spaces), which broke text matching and would show in exported lists. | Register a patient without a middle name and open the search results. | `patient_full_name()` joined first, middle and last name with fixed spaces even when the middle name was empty. | Empty parts are now skipped when the name is built. | Fixed, E2E-025 to E2E-027 pass |
| DEF-004 | 5 | Screenshot review | Low | Table columns (Actions, Details, Expiry) were cut off, and outpatient numbers, dates and amounts wrapped onto several lines, on a 960-pixel screen. | Open User accounts, Audit trail or the Stock report in a browser window 960 pixels wide. | Wide tables with generous padding and wrapping numbers. | Table padding and text size reduced; outpatient numbers, dates and amounts kept on one line; account buttons may wrap. Screenshots retaken and all suites re-run. | Fixed, screenshots checked; 167, 185 and 185 tests pass |
| DEF-005 | 6 | UT-DSH-015 | Low | The daily collections series returned the whole number 0 instead of 0.0 for a day with no money once refunds were taken into account (the chart code expects numbers of one type). | Run UT-DSH-015 after adding the refund-aware series. | `max(0, $v)` returns its first argument, an integer, when the two are equal. | The series is converted with `max(0.0, (float) $v)`. UT-DSH-015 and UT-RFD-014 pass. | Fixed |
| DEF-006 | 6 | E2E-448 | Low | The printed copy of a patient record left out the reason of a refund request, although the downloaded file had it. | Give a copy of a record that has a refund request and compare the page with the JSON file. | The HTML table was built with fewer columns than the data. | A Reason column was added to the printed table. E2E-448 passes. | Fixed |
| DEF-007 | 6 | E2E-476 | Low | The copy page had no main heading (`h1`), which breaks the one-heading-per-page rule the other pages follow and makes the page harder to use with a screen reader. | Open a copy of a record and look for `h1`. | The page asked for no title because the copy has its own header. | The page now shows the standard title. E2E-476 passes. | Fixed |
| DEF-008 | 6 | E2E-466 (test fault) | Low | A check that an erased patient cannot be found failed even though the patient was not listed. | Search for the old name of an erased patient. | The search box repeats what was typed, so the name appeared on the page. | The check now looks for the patient's link and name in the results, not the whole page. No application change. | Fixed (test) |
| DEF-009 | 6 | Browser check at 390 and 320 pixels | Low | Several pages (invoices, reports, user accounts) could be dragged sideways on a phone although the tables have their own scroll box. | Open an invoice with payments in a browser window 390 pixels wide and scroll sideways. | Visually hidden text (the Actions column heading) is positioned absolutely, and with no positioned parent it was placed against the page instead of the scrolling table box, so it widened the page. Grid cells could also grow wider than the screen. | `.table-wrap` and `.mini-wrap` are now positioned, grid cells may shrink (`min-width: 0`), and the invoice tables are wrapped like the others. Checked at 1440, 390 and 320 pixels with no sideways scroll; not covered by an automated test. | Fixed, browser check |
